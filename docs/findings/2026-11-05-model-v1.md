## Change from the joint-fit draft

A first draft fitted era levels and instructor effects jointly. For CMPSC 24 it put the 2009–14 era level at 53% although all 14 offerings in that era were between 20% and 55%, and attributed the gap to the instructors who taught then. With era levels computed from the offerings directly, the 2009–14 level is 40% and the CMPSC 24 score fell from 0.44 to 0.05. See `docs/methodology.md` §1 for why the conservative version is used.
